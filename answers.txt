Q1: Why might we consider using Git?

Answer: Because it allows us to track changes in our code(so for example if we wanna revert back to old versions, see previous changes, etc.) safeguard data integrity, and have seamless collaboration

Q3: What is the difference between Merging and Rebasing?

Answer: Merging preserves the original history of changes whereas rebase completely rewrites the history to create a more linear timeline. Rebase makes the project history easier to read, but it has the potential to cause issues for your teammates by changing commit IDs
